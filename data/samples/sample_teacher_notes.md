# Sample Teacher Notes

## Classroom Goal

Use one original excerpt and one regenerated excerpt to help students observe:

- how style labels affect musical imagination
- how emotional tags shape aesthetic interpretation
- how visual prompts support comparative appreciation

## Suggested Questions

1. 哪些情绪标签最能描述原始音频？
2. 修改风格提示词后，音乐的气质发生了什么变化？
3. 视觉描述和你对音乐的主观感受是否一致？
